START WITH THE BASICS

Gear Up

You don't need a lot of equipment to start playing. A few essentials will help you feel ready for your first game.

Your Paddle

Choose a comfortable paddle that feels balanced in your hand. Beginners can start with an affordable recreational model.

Court Shoes

Supportive court shoes provide traction for quick lateral movements. Choose footwear designed for the surface you play on.

Your Pickleball

Pick a ball designed for pickleball. Outdoor balls are generally harder and have smaller holes than indoor balls.

KNOW BEFORE YOU PLAY

The Rules

Learn a few key rules before stepping onto the court. They will help you understand how a typical rally works.

  • Serving

    Serve diagonally into the opposite service court. Traditional serving rules require an underhand motion or a drop serve, depending on the method used.

  • The Double-Bounce Rule

    After the serve, the receiving team must let the ball bounce before returning it. The serving team must then let that return bounce before hitting it.

  • The Kitchen

    The non-volley zone near the net is commonly called the kitchen. You cannot volley the ball while touching this zone, including its boundary line.

  • Scoring

    Traditional side-out scoring awards points to the serving team. Many recreational games are played to 11 points, with a two-point winning margin. Check which format your group uses.
